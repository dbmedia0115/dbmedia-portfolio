Privacy

Your details and your photos

Dream Big Media is run by Samuel Agbemadji, a photographer and videographer based in Nottingham, UK. This page explains what I collect, why, and how long I keep it. Questions or requests: db.media0115@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Enquiries: the name, email and shoot details you send through the contact form.
  • Client galleries: your name and email before you download, which photos you favourite, and basic visit records (when the gallery was opened and what was downloaded) so I can help if something goes wrong.
  • Print requests and reviews: the details you enter, such as name, email, phone (optional), print choices and your review.

Why I use it

  • To reply to you, deliver your photos and films, and handle print orders. That's the work you've asked me to do.
  • To show your review on my website, only if you ticked the box allowing it, and with your first name only.
  • To send you occasional news and offers, only if you ticked that box. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing me.

I never sell your details or share them for anyone else's marketing.

Who helps me run this

Your details and files are stored with trusted services that run the website: Supabase (database), Cloudflare R2 (gallery files), Vercel (website hosting) and Web3Forms (form emails). Google Analytics measures anonymous site visits.

How long I keep things

  • Gallery photos and films are deleted from the website 90 days after delivery. Please download everything you want to keep.
  • Gallery visit records are cleared after 180 days.
  • Names and emails from galleries, print requests and reviews are kept while you're a client and for up to 2 years after, unless you ask me to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details I hold about you, or to stop any marketing, at any time. Just email db.media0115@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
